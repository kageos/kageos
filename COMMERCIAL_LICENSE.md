## Commercial license categories

### Hosted Platform License

For providing external customers with hosted access to kageos workspaces,
Service Trees, directory creation or installation, agents, application
building, platform administration, or substantially similar functionality.

### Managed Service Provider License

For an MSP, agency, consultant, or operator that hosts or controls kageos
instances for one or more external customers as its own managed service.
Consulting on a customer-controlled deployment does not require this license
when the customer's use remains within the default BSL grant.

### OEM and Embed License

For embedding, white-labeling, rebranding, or OEM-distributing the kageos core
platform as a core value-driving component of another product or service.
Independently developed directories, applications, plugins, and integrations
that use the Apache-2.0 kageos SDK do not require this license unless they
incorporate the core platform or provide a substantially similar platform.

### Commercial On-Premises Distribution License

For supplying a packaged, modified, rebranded, or production-ready kageos core
platform to third parties for their production use as part of a commercial
offering.

## Uses that normally do not need a commercial license

- Personal use and internal production use.
- Internal use shared among Affiliates under common control.
- Public forms, portals, reports, dashboards, APIs, notifications,
  automations, and support interfaces that satisfy the Customer-Facing
  Solution conditions in the license.
- Implementation, integration, configuration, training, and support for an
  instance controlled by the customer.
- Commercial directories, applications, plugins, and integrations developed
  against the public kageos SDK or public interfaces without incorporating the
  core platform or substituting for it.

## Requesting commercial authorization

Email [admin@kageos.ai](mailto:admin@kageos.ai) with the subject
`[kageos commercial license]` and include:

1. Your legal entity and primary contact.
2. The product or service you plan to offer.
3. Who will control the deployment and infrastructure.
4. What external users can see, create, configure, or administer.
5. Whether kageos will be hosted, modified, embedded, white-labeled,
   rebranded, or distributed on premises.
6. Expected regions, customer count or deployment scale, and target launch
   date.

The maintainers may request architecture or licensing details needed to define
the correct authorization. Sending a request does not itself grant additional
rights. Additional rights begin only when a separate written commercial
agreement is signed by the parties.
