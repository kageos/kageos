# Code Of Conduct

> 状态：执行口径
> 更新时间：2026-05-17
> 负责人窗口：事项 1 / codex/open-source-governance

We want AI-Agent-OS to be a practical, respectful place to build.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific in technical disagreement.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing clear reproduction steps and context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers ask to refocus.
- Using the project spaces to promote unrelated products or campaigns.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project healthy.
